## Main cast

Player codes: `{px}` full name, `{pm}` surname, `{pn}` given name (glossary.md style rules).

- **Protagonist** (`{px}`, player-named, default Kazuki Shichidai): rookie Card Sealer of the National Diet Library
  Division (OXAS Japan), sent undercover as a transfer student into Class 3-2 at Karasu no Mori Academy. Lives at the
  Hatori home. Wears a single glove over his Card Sealer's Mark (ms05_00:219:00). Silent; speaks only through choices.
- **Toji Dan**: classmate, brawler with a code ("as long as you've got a good reason, there's no fear, no mercy and
  no defeat", ms01_01:079:00). Rough and clipped ("ain't", "y'know", "c'mon"). Calls the hero his partner. Clashes with Tomoe.
- **Minori Hosaka**: classmate in the next seat, gentle, a little timid, loves ghost stories and the occult. Soft and
  polite ("Um...", "I wonder..."). Says "Dan-kun". Repeats "as long as we're all together, we'll be okay".
- **Tomoe Tobisaka**: student council president ("the boss around here", ms01_01:012:00), glasses, sharp-tongued and
  dutiful, softens toward the hero. Brisk ("Honestly!", "idiot" for Toji). Minori's best friend.
- **Mao** (白, the White Card): guardian spirit of the Curse Hanafuda who appears as a small girl once the hero draws her
  (ms01_03). Archaic and haughty ("I daresay", "'tis", "you dolt"); calls the hero "my master". Loves sweets, pizza-flavour snacks.
- **Zero Chiaki** (雉明零, read ぜろ, never "Rei"): passed the Card Sealer exam with the hero and Ichiru, then
  vanished (ms00_03). Actually split off from the Demon Card (鬼札, dg11_02:012:19); gains a "heart" through the hero.
  Worked for Banri, who taught him how to release the cards (dg13_01:013:01). Quiet, plain, earnest.
- **Ichiru Muto**: fellow rookie Card Sealer from the exam. Bubbly ("Ehehe", "kinda", "~"), says "Chiaki-kun". Sent
  to Kyoto for remedial study, returns in ch04, ch08-09, ch11.
- **Daisuke Isachi**: Chief of the NDL Collection Department's Special Duties Division; the hero's boss. Gruff mentor
  who insists he is not a teacher but answers to "sensei" (ms00_01:014:09). "You're the right one for it here. I guarantee it."
- **Asako Hatori**: the hero's homeroom teacher (Class 3-2), Seishiro's daughter; chronically late, flustered,
  sweet. Unknowingly of Kamo blood: the "true" Executor (dg11_01:012:00).
- **Seishiro Hatori**: priest of Karasuba Shrine, head of the Hatori family and heir to the Kamo line; the hero's host.
  Gruff ("Honestly", "idiot girl"). Arranged for the hero to stand in for Asako as Executor (dg11_01:002:00).
- **Hisae Makimura**: languid teacher and school librarian, teases ("How romantic"), knows more than she says;
  drives off JEMA agents in ch08.
- **Ken** and **Suzu**: Karasuba Shrine's divine messengers, invisible to people without the Eye. Ken: folksy retainer ("young master", "if you'd
  permit me"). Suzu: childlike, speaks of herself as "Suzu", says "Sei-sama", "it is" endings.
- **Nagahide Shishido**: second-year kendo captain, Toji's junior; Aki (Hiroshima) dialect ("ye", "I tell ya",
  "Osu!!"). Possessed in ch02; afterwards swears to follow the hero for life (ms12_00:237:01).
- **Shu Takarakata**: wears a paper bag, obsessed with squares, runs the Square Research Club. Halting speech broken
  by commas ("Squares are, the best").
- **Rin Himukai**: Yahiro middle-schooler, ninja of the Senka-ryu (Whirling Fire) school that once guarded the
  cards; brash ("Nothing is impossible for a Senka-ryu ninja!"). Calls Shibukawa "Master".
- **Ayahito Konosu**: Shinjuku information broker at the cafe; florid and masochistic ("beautiful ones", begs to be
  hit). Fond of Rin.
- **Shibukawa**: taciturn cafe owner, odd comma-heavy speech; keeps the long-eared dog Kanae.
- **Kalpataru** ("Kal-san"): self-styled Mysterious Indian Man who runs the curry shop. Mock-archaic: "verily",
  "thou/thy", "Achaa!". Menus: recommended / unrecommended / unrecommendable curry.
- **Togashi**: detective, Shinjuku Precinct (female); blunt, dutiful, blocked by superiors. Subordinate: Officer Sakaguchi.
- **Hideo Kamon**: Yahiro third-year, self-proclaimed Hero ("Ha ha ha!!", "justice!"). Also the Masked Man vigilante,
  passed off as "my master's elder brother's friend's..." (ms05_03:011:12).
- **Dokai Gamo** ("Mikamyun"): Yahiro third-year, cross-dressing fortune-teller of the esoteric arts; cutesy ("~",
  "a maiden's heart"). Calls Kaname "Kanamen".
- **Kaname Kotokuji**: Yahiro, guardian of the Winter Hollow, Kotokuji talisman arts; icy and politely cutting. At
  first calls the hero "not fit to be the Executor" (ms06_01:059:04).
- **Gio Onimaru**: Kosei High, boss of the Oni Mark Bandits, treasure-hunter. Calls himself "the great me" (俺様),
  "Oi oi oi oi!!". Rival who comes to respect the hero (ch10).
- **Angie**: Kosei High bandit posing as a Spanish exchange student; tarot reader, "bruja". Spanish asides
  ("Vaya", "senor", "si"), refers to herself as "Angie", ends lines with "ne". Calls Gio "Boss".
- **Migiri Kashima**: Kosei, the bandits' strategist; curt, "idiots", "small fry"; known as "Yuriwaka the Demon" (ms09_01:273:07).
- **Nobuyuki Chikushi**: man in the black coat; JEMA Assistant Director-General ("Second Lieutenant"), Isachi's old
  acquaintance. Grandiloquent ("Let the twilight begin--", ms00_01:000:28). Holds the Man Card; author of the Human
  Dragon Project.
- **Banri Takadono**: man in the white school uniform; seeks the Akashic Record's "evolution" of humanity. Lofty, fatalistic.
- **Benio Sawakami**: Banri's devoted swordswoman ("Nii-sama"), keeps the flying squirrel Tsukifune. Formal and
  quiet. Holds the Plum and Warbler card. Likely the girl in uniform in the prologue.
- Minor: the Shop Lady (school store), the gym teacher, Asako's late mother (could sense spirits).

## Factions and terms

- **OXAS**: international body that collects ORDS; its Japan branch is hidden inside the **National Diet Library**
  (Collection Department, Special Duties Division, called "the Division").
- **ORDS** (カミフダ): condensed information from antiquity, as cards, books or scraps (ms00_01:030:11). "ORDS" or "the cards".
- **Card Sealer** (封札師; also "Card Archiver"): ORDS-collecting agent certified by OXAS. Bears a **Card Sealer's Mark**.
- **Arcane Eye** (秘法眼): the sight that perceives ORDS; found via an odd school survey (ms00_01:014:00).
- **Curse Hanafuda** (呪言花札): 48 cards (12 months x 4), made by the Kamo onmyoji line, stolen and their seal broken.
  Grades: Bright, Animal, Ribbon, Chaff. Two guardian incarnations: **White Card** (Mao) and **Demon Card** (Zero).
- **Executor** (執行者): the one the cards choose to gather and seal them; sealing costs the Executor's life
  (ms09_01:149:63). Normally of Kamo blood.
- **Card-Possessed** (札憑き): a person possessed by a card; gains power, risks becoming an Oni. Mark of the Card-Possessed.
- **Oni** (隠人): monsters born of the cards' power; roam the Hollows.
- **Hollow** (洞): underground labyrinths on the **dragon vein** (龍脈). Four Seasons Hollows: Autumn (Karasu no Mori,
  under the old incinerator), Spring (Hanazono Shrine, ms03_02:037:00), Winter (Yahiro Academy), Summer (ch11). The
  **Ryujudo** (龍珠堂) lies at their centre, under Shinjuku Station.
- **Hermit's Mark** / **Hermit's Staff**: Chikushi's card-binding brand; Initiate (秘儀を伝授されし者) = Banri's term for the hero.
- **Oni Mark Bandits** (鬼印盗賊団): Kosei High gang under Gio; skull emblem.
- **JEMA** (Cabinet Emergency Management Office): Chikushi's agency; its mobile investigation unit raids the Division (ch08).
- **Yagari Agency** (夜狩機構): agency under the Imperial Household Agency, one of the "three agencies" (ms06_01:143:02).
- **Kosei High** (寇聖): new boarding school near Shinjuku Gyoen, red uniforms. **Yahiro Academy** (八汎): elite school, Kamon/Gamo/Kotokuji.
- Places: Karasuba Shrine (Hatori home), Hanazono Shrine, the cafe, the curry shop, Kabukicho, Shinjuku Central Park.

## Chapter summaries

### Prologue (ms00)
- Cold open: a librarian takes the hero's survey results ("May the truth... set you free--", ms00_01:000:05). In Shinjuku the
  man in the black coat (Chikushi) and a girl in uniform discuss the coming "Great Purification" (ms00_01:000:14);
  "Dusk swallows the city--" / "Let the twilight begin--" (ms00_01:000:08, :000:28).
- Aokigahara, Mt. Fuji: Ichiru finds the hero; Isachi explains ORDS and the Arcane Eye; Zero introduces himself (ms00_01:019:00).
- Wind cave exam with Division Test Cards; Isachi puts the hero in the lead: "You're the right one for it here. I
  guarantee it." (ms00_01:045:01), "We will now begin the Card Sealer certification exam--" (ms00_01:065:02).
- After a cave-in Zero is gone (ms00_03:000:01). An Oni is sighted at a Tokyo high school; Ichiru goes to Kyoto, the
  hero to "Shinjuku-- Karasu no Mori Academy" (ms00_03:021:01).

### Chapter 1 (ms01)
- First day: Hisae (librarian, "student and teacher, understood?"), Asako arrives late and takes homeroom; Minori
  sits next door. Tomoe introduces herself; Toji is the trouble she chases.
- Tomoe tells the incinerator ghost story (bones, a custodian who quit) and the new keyword "something white" (ms01_01:068:00).
- At Karasuba Shrine the hero meets Ken and Suzu and gruff Seishiro, Isachi's contact (ms01_01:162:00).
- Night: Toji, Minori and the hero open the incinerator, find the Hollow and draw the White Card: Mao, furious at
  being bound by a "heretical power" not of her masters' bloodline (ms01_03:011:09). Closing tag: "So the Card
  Sealer has drawn the White Card..." (ms01_03:023:01).

### Chapter 2 (ms02)
- The hero lives at the Hatori home as a "distant relative" with Seishiro, Asako (his teacher, ms02_00:040:08) and
  Mao; running breakfast gags (Japanese-style only, Mao's tastes) recur each chapter.
- Ken explains Mao and the ORDS; Mao: gather the cards your own way, she alone can tear them from their host (ms02_00:078:04).
- A kendo slasher; Shu appears; Kalpataru's curry shop debuts ("A Mysterious Indian Man, verily!!", ms02_01:080:02).
- Kendo captain Nagahide is Card-Possessed (water god at his family dojo); the party saves him in the Hollow. He
  swears loyalty (ms02_03:019:01). Someone runs off with a card; Mao names the Executor (ms02_03:031:09).

### Chapter 3 (ms03)
- A small figure tails the hero (Rin). Mao explains the 48 cards, 12 suits, four seasons and dragon veins (ms03_00:113:09).
- Hisae lectures on the academy's history and Tokyo's bond with fire (ms03_00:174:00).
- Detective Togashi questions the group (ms03_01:035:03); at the cafe they meet Shibukawa and Ayahito, who explains
  the Curse Hanafuda's history and the Senka-ryu (ms03_01:117:06) and names Migiri Kashima.
- Hanazono Shrine (Spring Hollow): ninja Rin hands her hanafuda to the hero (ms03_03:003:13); Mao warns that
  the cards' knowledge eats away at their holder until they stop being human (ms03_03:017:07).

### Chapter 4 (ms04)
- Ken: Lord Saionji entrusted the cards to the Senka-ryu (ms04_00:044:15). Talk of onmyoji and Abe no Seimei.
- Angie transfers into Class 1 ("Angie is still looking for a novio", ms04_00:134:15); tarot readings call the hero
  a "blank" (ms04_01:042:00). She lures him to the roof, charms him and demands his hanafuda; Toji and Tomoe break in.
- Ichiru returns and Isachi briefs the group: the Curse Hanafuda are the Division's oldest target (ms04_01:246:31).
  Chikushi passes them at the cafe (ms04_01:258:11).
- Skull-mark bandits steal Ayahito's card at Hanazono Shrine; recovered below. Zero reappears (ms04_03:028:14).

### Chapter 5 (ms05)
- The Masked Man foils the bandits (ms05_00:000:12). Chikushi talks with the hero at the shrine (ms05_00:052:03).
  In Central Park Zero gives the hero a Bright card (ms05_00:289:01; Mao: "You received it from him!?").
- Togashi joins the curry run; Migiri and Angie at the curry shop; Hideo Kamon debuts (ms05_00:217:02).
  Toji: "When it's time to fight, we fight together. --Don't forget it." (ms05_00:238:02).
- Gio and the Oni Mark Bandits raid the schoolyard (ms05_01:057:01); Angie is unmasked as Kosei. Hanazono Hollow
  again; Hideo covers for his "master's... master" (ms05_03:011:12) and invites them to Yahiro. On the City Hall deck
  an unnamed man (Banri, named in ch09) and Zero, who works for him, track the Bright cards (ms05_03:017:14-31).

### Chapter 6 (ms06)
- Isachi visits the shrine (ms06_00:027:01). Hideo challenges the group to a race to his school.
- Yahiro Academy: gatekeeper Dokai "Mikamyun" Gamo, then Kaname Kotokuji, guardian of the Winter Hollow: the
  Kamo onmyoji made the cards, and a mere Card Sealer is unfit as Executor (ms06_01:048:07, :059:04).
- Ayahito explains the three agencies, incl. JEMA and the Yagari Agency (ms06_01:143:02).
- Bathhouse episode (Asako, Hisae, Tomoe, Rin, Gio, Migiri, Ayahito); Dokai, the cross-dresser, flees with a card.
  Hideo vouches for him (ms06_01:385:17). Kaname shows Kotokuji talisman arts (ms06_02:027:13).

### Chapter 7 (ms07)
- Kosei turns on Gio ("Pathetic, Gio... That we'd betray you", ms07_00:000:05).
- Asako is ill; Hisae hints at the shrine's lost history and that the family hides something "out of kindness"
  (ms07_00:115:01). Asako collapses in gym; nurse's office visits; she recalls her mother's death (ms07_00:351:02).
- Minori admits she fears the hero will disappear once the cards are gathered (ms07_00:332:01).
- Minori goes missing; the police are told to sit on it (ms07_01:125:26). Gio, beaten by his own men, joins;
  the trail leads under Yahiro (ms07_01:163:06). Chikushi orders a 0520 raid (ms07_03:000:13).

### Chapter 8 (ms08)
- JEMA under Chikushi seizes the Division under the Subversive Activities Prevention Act; Isachi is sidelined
  (ms08_00:000:03-44). Chikushi calls the hero a human SACRIFICE (ms08_00:000:30).
- Seishiro snaps at the hero; Ken hints Asako's sickness has a clear cause (ms08_00:070:01).
- JEMA agents corner the hero at school; Ichiru crashes in; Hisae holds them off: "I hope you find a Treasure of
  truth that's yours alone." (ms08_00:191:11).
- Hideout in the home ec room: the hero escapes the locked-down school in a girls' sailor uniform (ms08_03).
  Ichiru wants to bury the cards and quit (ms08_03:093:42).

### Chapter 9 (ms09)
- On the run: Togashi defies her orders (ms09_01:025:13); cafe and curry shop rally Rin, Ayahito, Angie, Migiri.
- Yahiro tea room: Kaname and Dokai explain the Four Seasons Hollows and Chikushi's family (ms09_01:149:22);
  Ichiru reveals the Executor's fate is death for the seal (ms09_01:149:63). Gio: "Treasure don't choose the hands
  of somebody who's scared." (ms09_01:170:06).
- Everyone storms the NDL basement. Banri introduces himself (ms09_01:358:05, "eleven years after this world chose to endure").
- dg09: Chikushi reveals the Human Dragon Project (dragon vein reversed onto Fuji, two-thirds of the population
  lost, dg09_01:010:12, :030:11); Benio cuts him down. Isachi: "Come back alive, no matter what. ...That's an
  order." (dg09_01:030:21). Chikushi survives (ms09_03:000:07).

### Chapter 10 (ms10, dg10)
- Mostly dungeon. Gio calls the hero out to the Hollow where they first met for a winner-takes-the-treasure duel
  (ms10_00:000:02, dg10_00:000:00). Card-Possessed, he asks to be killed before he turns; the hero saves him. He
  leaves to hunt "some other treasure" but returns for the finale (dg10_01:039:03).

### Chapter 11 (ms11, dg11)
- Migiri hands over his card, Angie says goodbye ("Que te vaya bien", ms11_00:017:06). Seishiro apologises and
  asks the hero to look after Asako (ms11_00:051:00).
- Christmas Eve: Zero asks "The one person precious above all, or a crowd of strangers?" (ms11_00:078:19).
  Toji: "I'm... never givin' up. Not till the very end." (ms11_01:129:01). Christmas party in 3-2; Tomoe: "As
  long as I'm around, I won't let you do anything reckless." (ms11_02:066:02). Mao: "Is there something you wish
  to protect?" (ms11_02:122:01).
- Benio attacks the shrine, wounds Seishiro, takes Asako into the Summer Hollow (ms11_02:137:07).
- dg11: Benio reveals the hero was chosen to die as a stand-in for Asako, the true Executor (dg11_01:002:00, :012:00);
  Asako's ring suppresses her blood. Seishiro intervenes. Zero, born from the Demon Card, fights the hero to
  destroy the cards so he survives (dg11_02:012:19, :035:09 "I believe that you-- can bring me to an end").
  Only two years of ki remain (dg11_03:014:18).
- Final choice dg11_03: Seal / Destroy / Think of another way. One branch leads to the **bad end MS11_04**;
  "another way" (neither release nor a sealing that costs a life, dg11_03:022:03) leads on to ch12. Which choice
  triggers the bad end has not been checked in the emulator.

### Chapter 12 (ms12, dg12)
- Aftermath: the ki flow is restored, Asako recovers and remembers Mao and Zero from childhood ("I'm sorry I forgot
  you for so long", ms12_00:069:05). Zero is with them at the shrine; Ken: the hero has written new words into the cards'
  history (ms12_00:048:24).
- Closing ceremony day: one-on-one scenes (Toji on the roof "this is my path", Minori on graduation, Shu, Nagahide
  "I, Nagahide Shishido, will follow ya for the rest of my life", Tomoe).
- Tsukifune leads the group under the school; Benio asks for a final duel, then asks the hero to save Banri, who seeks
  Akashic Record "evolution" via the dragon-vein power; Ryujudo lies beneath Shinjuku Station (dg12_01:024:00).

### Chapter 13 (ms13, dg13)
- Cleaning up after skipping the ceremony; sunset on the roof with everyone; Hisae sends them off (ms13_01:036:00).
- Shinjuku Station underpass; Benio guides them to the Ryujudo; every ally assembles (dg13_00). Mao: "{px}, you are...
  the finest master I could have." (ms13_02:008:02).
- Banri is defeated and offers to act as regulating valve (dg13_03:008:04); the power is shared among everyone in the
  Hollow; one line per ally (dg13_03:022:00-048:01). Tomoe makes the hero promise lunch, then dinner (dg13_04:024:02).
- Final boss: the metallic voice, Thrice-Great keeper of the Akashic Record and Creator of the ORDS (dg13_02:013:14),
  severs the cards from the Executor and absorbs Mao; the hero and Zero pull her back. The cards renounce their
  Creator and give the hero "the power to slay even a god" (dg13_12:011:04).

## Endings

- **Bad end (MS11_04)**: the Initiate "ascended to Adept" and the power was loosed into the heavens (ms11_04:001:00);
  Banri foretells humanity's "evolution" in 17,520 hours (two years); Benio chooses to stay with him even if she stops
  being human; news reports of drying lakes and riots.
- **True end (MS13_03)**: Mao and Zero, changed, hear the voices calling them back: "To that place, where our
  beloved ones are waiting------" (ms13_03:000:20). Keep "This Heart" capitalised as in ms13_03:000:15.
